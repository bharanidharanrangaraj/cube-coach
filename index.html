Private by design

The camera picture is read right here on this device, a few times a second, and thrown away. Nothing is uploaded: there is no server. Your progress is kept in this browser only.

Use it with no internet

Open Cube Coach once from a web address that starts with https://, then use your browser's Install app or Add to Home screen. After that it opens from your home screen and works in airplane mode.

On a computer you can also just double-click index.html.

Tips for good scans

  • Even light from above or behind you. Avoid a lamp shining on the stickers (glare).
  • Fill the grid with one face. Keep the cube still until the grid turns green.
  • Front camera (laptop or selfie): the picture is mirrored, like a mirror. That's fine.
